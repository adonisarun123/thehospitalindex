# 2026-11-25: merge duplicate places

Applied directly to production (Neon project `patient-boat-94859550`, branch `br-summer-violet-bicnguxj`) in two transactions.
Rollback point: snapshot `pre-geo-merge-2026-11-25` (`snap-fragrant-rain-bi6el3rb`).

## What a merge does

For each `from → to` pair (same state, same kind):

- every branch (any status) on `from` moves to `to`; search rows rebuilt with `refresh_branch_search`;
- localities, child settlements, pincodes, hub content and catchments move to `to`;
- `from`'s name and slug, and its aliases, become `common_spelling` aliases of `to`, so the old URL
  308s to the new one through `resolveChild` and the importer (`import_scrape.py`) maps the old
  spelling to `to` on the next import;
- `from` is set `is_active = false`. Its slug stays reserved.

Only spelling variants and old/new names of the same place were merged. Ambiguous pairs were left alone:
Delhi / New Delhi and the Delhi zones, a district name standing in for a town (East Godavari, Gautam Buddha Nagar,
Kamrup, SBS Nagar, SAS Nagar, Ernakulam vs Kochi), Ambala / Ambala City, Navi Mumbai, Greater Noida.

## Missing districts filled

114 branches (64 published) had a town but no district. Each got the district that every other record for its town
agrees on (the town's own district, or the single district all its other branches carry). Towns that span several
districts (e.g. Hyderabad) were not filled: about 1,090 published branches still have no district.
